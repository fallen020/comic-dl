## What comic-dl is

comic-dl is a general-purpose downloader for comic and manga galleries. It
reads the metadata and image links that a site's own pages expose publicly,
downloads the images, and packages them into CBZ, ZIP, and CBT archives. It
is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or authorized by any of the sites it
supports.

## Use it for content you may access

Download only content you own, are authorized to download, or are otherwise
legally permitted to access under the site's terms and your local law. This
page does not define what that is for you — your jurisdiction, the site's
terms, and the specific work all matter.

## Copyright

Creating, retaining, or sharing offline copies of copyrighted content may
infringe copyright, even if you can view the content online. Having saved a
copy is not a defense to infringement. The substantial-non-infringing-uses
doctrine shows why a tool like this one is not *itself* illegal, but it says
nothing about whether any particular download by a user is legal; see
[Sony v. Universal (Betamax)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Corp._of_America_v._Universal_City_Studios,_Inc.)
and
[MGM v. Grokster (inducement)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MGM_Studios,_Inc._v._Grokster,_Ltd.).

## Terms of service

Scraping and automation are frequently prohibited by a site's terms of
service even where they are not unlawful. Paywall bypass is not a supported
use of comic-dl. Similar tools frame the same stance explicitly — see
[gallery-dl's terms of service](https://gallery-dl.wiki/terms-of-service)
("no paywall bypass", user responsibility).

## Access controls and the law

comic-dl does not bypass access controls by default: challenge solving is
opt-in (`solver = "off"`), and enabling a solver is an explicit choice per
run or in config. Circumventing technical protection measures can be
unlawful:

- In the US, the DMCA §1201 prohibits circumventing access controls — see
  [the statute](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap12.html).
- Unauthorized access to a computer system can implicate the US CFAA.
- Other jurisdictions — including Japan, Korea, and the EU — have their own
  anti-circumvention and computer-access rules; they differ from the US.

## How similar tools fared

- In 2020 GitHub reinstated youtube-dl after a DMCA §1201 takedown,
  concluding the tool had substantial non-infringing uses. See GitHub's
  [statement](https://github.blog/2020-11-16-standing-up-for-developers-youtube-dl-is-back/)
  and the [EFF's analysis](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2020/11/dmca-takedown-youtube-dl-coders-bad).
- GitHub's own [DMCA Takedown Policy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/other-site-policies/dmca-takedown-policy)
  explains how takedown notices and counter-notices work.
